Banana Smoothie Recipe

Home

A banana smoothie is a sweet and refreshing drink made with fresh bananas and milk. It is creamy, delicious, and perfect for breakfast or a quick snack.

Ingedients

  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 cup of mil
  • 1 1/5 tsp honey

Steps

  1. Peel the banana and cut it into pieces.
  2. Put the banana pieces into a blender
  3. Add the milk and honey
  4. Blend until smooth and creamy
  5. Pour into a glass and enjoy!
