Scrambled eggs Recipe

Home

Scrambled eggs are a delicious and easy breakfast made with eggs, butter, and a little salt. They are soft, fluffy, and perfect for a quick morning meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp of butter
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Crack eggs into a bowl.
  2. Mix with a fork
  3. Melt butter in a pan.
  4. Add eggs and stir until cooked
