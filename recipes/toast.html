Butter Toast recipe

Home

Toast is a simple and quick breakfast made by heating slices of bread until they become golden and crispy. It tastes great with butter and takes only a few minutes to prepare.

Ingediends

  • 2 slices of bread
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Place the bread slices in a toaster.
  2. Toast until golden and crispy.
  3. Spread butter on the warm toast.
